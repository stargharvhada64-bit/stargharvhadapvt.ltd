About Us

Welcome to Star GharVhada

A modern approach to property rental and management in Nepal.

Making Property Management Simple

Star GharVhada Pvt. Ltd. is being developed as a professional property management and rental service company in Nepal.

Our concept is to connect property owners and tenants while helping manage the daily responsibilities associated with rental properties.

Our services are designed around tenant management, rent management, maintenance coordination, property inspection, tenant placement and regular communication with property owners.
